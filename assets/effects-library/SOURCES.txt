Other imagery is designed motion graphics made with Kinetic Cut's existing
Qt Quick / Direct3D 11 motion engine. Icons use the bundled Lucide icon set.
Typography uses the bundled OFL Inter font. There are no model downloads.

The MP4 examples are silent, 384x216 at 30 fps. Each plays only on request.
Previews demonstrate existing rendering/defaults; focal markers and specific
sample targets are presentation assets, not changes to the effect algorithms.
Static effects, analysis/actions and the Text/Headline titles have thumbnails
without a Play button. The catalogue/manifest maps all existing effect names.
